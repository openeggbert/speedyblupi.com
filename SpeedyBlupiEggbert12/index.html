Speedy Blupi & Eggbert

All four reconstructed games, running in your browser through Free API and Free Direct.

Speedy Blupi 1 US · 1998 Speedy Eggbert 1 English · eGames Speedy Blupi 2 English Speedy Eggbert 2 English · eGames

Choose a game and press Start game. Graphics, levels, sound effects and music are included.

A browser with WebAssembly threads is required. Hosting must provide COOP and COEP headers; use the included server locally.
